# Artwork attribution

`app/art/coffer.png` is the Coffer mark supplied by the Coffer team and reused
with the same attribution as the DOOM badge project. Its source is the team's
186 × 230 [SVG source](https://github.com/coffer-so/doom-port-to-bp-badge/blob/main/assets/coffer-export.svg).
The cream mark is exported to a transparent 22 × 27 PNG and displayed at scale 1.
This is approximately 15% smaller per dimension than the earlier 26 × 32 display.
The branding retains its owner's rights; the source-code licenses do not relicense
the Coffer mark.

Avatars are fetched at runtime from public profile/image services. No actual
user avatar, public-profile response, Wi-Fi credential, console log or device
capture is part of the app or its synthetic test fixtures.
